ASSUMPTIONS AND LOCKED PARAMETERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Detector : Excelitas C30737LH-500-90 Si APD, active dia 0.5 mm (area 0.196 mm^2), Ct = 1.0 pF, cutoff 380 MHz
           (>> TIA bandwidth, so APD transit/cutoff not modelled).
Responsivity at M=1 (datasheet spectral curve): 0.55 A/W @900 nm,
           ~0.50 A/W @740 nm, ~0.37 A/W @630 nm.
Dark current: 0.1 nA typ AT OPERATING GAIN (multiplied/output);
           primary dark current used in shot noise = 0.1 nA / M.
Excess noise: McIntyre F = k*M + (2-1/M)*(1-k), k = 0.02 (Si).
           (Simple F = M^x, x = 0.3 would give F(100) = 4.0 vs
           3.95 here -- immaterial.)  Check: dark shot-noise
           density at M=100 from this model = 0.113 pA/rtHz vs datasheet 0.1 pA/rtHz at M~100 -- agreement OK.
TIA      : TI OPA657, en = 4.8 nV/rtHz, in = 1.3 fA/rtHz,
           GBP = 1.6 GHz, single-pole model with A0 = 70 dB
           (assumed), f_dom = GBP/A0.  Numeric integration
           0..3 GHz (numpy trapz, 30001 points).
Cin total: 2.3 pF  (APD Ct 1.0 pF, OPA657 input 0.8 pF, stray/layout 0.5 pF).  Cf is separate.
Baseline : Rf = 3300 ohm, Cf_total = 0.45 pF incl. parasitics (doc 02 locked).
Post amp : THS4304 x5, jumper selectable -- modelled at x1 AND x5.
Link     : OOK/NRZ at 50 Mbps (UI = 20 ns), extinction ratio
           10 dB ASSUMED; P1 = 2*Pavg*er/(er+1), P0 = P1/er;
           Q = (I1-I0)/(sigma1+sigma0); BER = 0.5*erfc(Q/sqrt(2)).
           Q targets: BER 1e-6 -> Q = 4.753; BER 1e-9 -> Q = 5.998.
Noise    : (a) en x noise-gain transfer |H_en(f)| integrated;
           (b) Rf Johnson sqrt(4kT/Rf) through |Zt(f)| integrated;
           (c) APD shot per level, 2q(Iph,pri+Id,pri)M^2 F * Beff,
               Beff from the integrated |Zt(f)| shape;
           (d) comparator noise: 0.1 mV rms ALLOWANCE at the
               comparator plane (not a datasheet number) --
               negligible vs the TIA noise below; post-amp noise
               is lumped into this allowance (model limitation).
           T = 290 K.

3) SENSITIVITY AT THE DESIGN POINT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chosen design: Rf = 3300 ohm, Cf = 0.45 pF, M = 100, f-3dB = 138.2 MHz
  (locked doc-02 design RETAINED: it passes R2 at its optimal M, so the fallback search was not used to change the design)
 lambda  R0 (A/W)   sens@BER1e-6   sens@BER1e-9
  740nm      0.50       -45.62 dBm       -44.31 dBm
  630nm      0.37       -44.31 dBm       -43.00 dBm
At the R2 point (-34 dBm, 740 nm, M = 100): signal (I1-I0) = 107.5 mV at TIA output,
  sigma1 = 2.86 mV, sigma0 = 1.06 mV, Q = 27.42, BER = 10^-165.1.
  Margin vs R2: 11.6 dB (sensitivity is that far below the -34 dBm requirement).

Post-stage cases (THS4304), at design M, 740 nm, BER 1e-6:
  post gain x1: sensitivity = -45.62 dBm (delta vs x1 = +0.000 dB)
  post gain x5: sensitivity = -45.66 dBm (delta vs x1 = -0.046 dB)
  Why so small: post gain multiplies signal and TIA/shot noise
  together; only the comparator-plane allowance (0.1 mV) is
  divided by the gain when input-referred.  The real value of
  the x5 stage is comparator overdrive: at sensitivity the
  half-swing at the comparator is 3.7 mV direct,
  18.5 mV with x5 -- relevant to the <20 mV-overdrive dispersion risk
  noted in doc 02.  At -34 dBm the direct half-swing is
  54 mV, comfortably above that risk zone.

5) SWING, CLIPPING AND OVERLOAD (+0 dBm, R3)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIA output bias 1.0 V, usable swing 3.5 Vpp (+/-1.75 V) on a 5 V rail.
    M  Pavg at clip (dBm)   level-1 demand at +0 dBm
   20               -15.3                     60 V (rails at 1.75 V)
   50               -19.3                    150 V (rails at 1.75 V)
  100               -22.3                    300 V (rails at 1.75 V)
  150               -24.1                    450 V (rails at 1.75 V)
  200               -25.3                    600 V (rails at 1.75 V)
At the design M = 100: linear operation ends at -22.3 dBm average;
+0 dBm is ~19 dB above that.  CONCLUSION, stated plainly: at
+0 dBm the TIA output WILL rail at any usable M -- clipping is
certain, not marginal.  That is normal for a receiver with
~12 dB of sensitivity margin below -34 dBm and a 3.3k TIA on
5 V; R3 does not require linear operation at +0 dBm, it
requires NO DAMAGE and recovery within 10 UI (200 ns).
Damage: no mechanism is identified in this budget -- the APD
current is bounded by the HV supply's hardware current limit
(doc 02: <200 uA class; bias collapses and gain falls under
strong light, which is self-limiting), and the TIA input node
is a virtual earth with the feedback network as the only path.
Recovery <= 10 UI is NOT PROVEN by this link budget: it is set
by the OPA657's overload recovery, the AC-coupling/baseline-
restore time constants and HV-rail recovery, none of which a
noise/link budget can establish.  It must be measured on the
bench (or in an overload transient simulation) before R3 is
claimed.  Treat R3 recovery as OPEN, not passed.

7) HV RIPPLE -> GAIN MODULATION (R9: ripple <= 20 mVpp)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gain law M = 1/(1-(V/Vbr)^n), n = 3, Vbr = 220 V.  d(lnM)/dV = n*u/(V*(1-u)), u = (V/Vbr)^n = 1 - 1/M.
  M =  50: V_op = 218.5 V (Vbr - V = 1.5 V), d(lnM)/dV = 0.673 /V, 20 mVpp ripple -> dM/M = 1.35 % pk-pk (+/-0.67 %)
  M = 100: V_op = 219.3 V (Vbr - V = 0.7 V), d(lnM)/dV = 1.355 /V, 20 mVpp ripple -> dM/M = 2.71 % pk-pk (+/-1.35 %)
At design M = 100: peak AM depth 1.35 % of the signal, i.e. a
  worst-case sensitivity loss of 0.118 dB, vs a 1-sigma noise fraction
  of 3.6 % at the R2 point and an R2 margin of 11.6 dB.  Ripple-induced AM is well below
  the noise: PASS.  (Criterion: peak AM << 1/Q fraction and << 5%.)
  Same sensitivity coefficient, for temperature: at fixed bias
  d(lnM) = 1.35 * 1.3 V/degC = 1.76 per degC -- i.e. fixed-bias
  gain is unusable across temperature at this M; the R9
  temperature compensation (bias tracking Vbr) is mandatory,
  as doc 02 already provides for.  This is also why the design
  M is the knee (~100), not the sweep edge (~200, where the
  ripple AM would be ~5.4 % pk-pk).
HV RIPPLE VERDICT: PASS (at design M, with temp compensation fitted)

8) COVERAGE SANITY CHECK vs THE ORIGINAL PAPER SYSTEM  [INFO]
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Paper benchmark: PER test, packet = 40,256 bits, packet fails if
any bit differs; PER = 1e-3, frame = 8 packets -> frame loss
  1-(1-1e-3)^8 = 0.797 %.  One SiPM receiver
covered ~700 cm^2 of lateral area (equivalent diameter
  2*sqrt(700/pi) = 29.9 cm).  Note that packet PER 1e-3
  corresponds to BER ~ 2.5e-08 (any-bit-fails over 40,256 bits),
  which is STRICTER than our R2 BER 1e-6 operating point.
Our estimate (free space, on axis, simple model):
  Emitter: NIR LED radiant intensity 1.7 mW/sr typ @ 20 mA
           (datasheet, paper); pulsed by the GaN driver --
           ASSUMED 5 mW/sr pulsed.  E = I/d^2;  P_rx = E * A_eff.
  Sensitivity used: -45.62 dBm = 27.4 nW (740 nm, BER 1e-6, design M).
  BARE: A_eff = 0.196 mm^2 -> on-axis range to sensitivity = 19 cm.
        At a 0.5 m mounting height the on-axis received power is -54.1 dBm,
        i.e. 8.4 dB BELOW sensitivity: bare mode does not cover a
        0.5 m geometry at all.  Bare mode is a bench / short-
        range mode (~20 cm class), as its -34 dBm R2 point implies.
  LENS: 25 mm lens, geometric 491 mm^2 x 0.9 transmission x 0.1
        coupling/defocus efficiency (ASSUMED) = 44.2 mm^2 effective -> on-axis
        range to sensitivity = 2.84 m.
  At receiver height 0.5 m with an assumed +/-15 deg useful cone: radius 13.4 cm,
  area = pi r^2 = 564 cm^2.  Range is NOT the limit inside the cone:
  at the cone edge (slant 0.52 m) P_rx = -30.8 dBm, still 14.8 dB above
  sensitivity (1/d^2 only; emitter off-axis falloff not modelled).
  Even at the stricter paper-equivalent BER (~2.5e-8, between
  our 1e-6 and 1e-9 sensitivities), the lens range stays >2 m, so
  the cone area at 0.5 m height is unchanged.
COMPARISON [INFO, not a gate]: lens-mode cone area 564 cm^2 vs the paper's
  ~700 cm^2 per SiPM receiver -- same class, 0.81x the area, under much cruder optics (one 25 mm
  lens vs a large-area SiPM).  CAVEAT, honestly: this is a pure
  geometric line-of-sight estimate.  The paper's system also
  collects diffuse/scattered light in an enclosed maze, and
  animal head orientation / emitter pattern dominate in
  practice; the geometric comparison understates BOTH systems'
  real coverage and must not be read as a measured coverage.
