Reading: on the dirty v5 board the boxed pads' escape corridors are
occupied by copper placed under v5's looser effective rules — the
repair-on-v5 trial therefore underestimates v6, and the decisive
test is the clean full-board route (power nets route early, while
their corridors are still empty).

Second finding (drove the SAFE fix in router6): the perpendicular
escape of a 0.5 mm-pitch power pin is EXACTLY clearance-wide —
pad-axis to neighbour pad edge = 0.35 mm = 0.10 neck half-width +
0.25 power clearance, a legal gap-equal-to-clearance route. U14.9's
+0.000 direction is that case. Any positive safety margin in the
router makes such pins unroutable by construction; router6's
acceptance threshold is now zero (1 um float-dust slack, the same
tolerance the audit itself applies).
